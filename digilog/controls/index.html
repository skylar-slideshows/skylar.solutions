Controls board

The knobs, buttons and faders for four channel strips

Try the control surface

The four channel strips, seen from above. Drag a knob up or down, or scroll over it, to turn it; hold Shift for finer steps, and double-click to reset it. The LED rings work the way the firmware draws them.

Design notes

  • Four channel strips, each with ten EC11 encoders with push switches, ten buttons, status LEDs and a screen.
  • Every encoder goes through an RC debounce filter to the MCP23017 I/O expanders: three per strip, with one I2C bus per strip.
  • LED rings: each is a 24 mm, four-layer board with 32 0201 LEDs and four 74HCT595 shift registers. With eight more 595s for the button LEDs, the whole surface is one 1344-bit chain, refreshed over SPI with DMA at 120 Hz.
  • Each ring plugs into two sockets: DATA (data, clock, latch and output enable) and POW (power, ground and cascade out). The LED resistors went from 560 Ω to 2.5 kΩ, since 560 Ω was far too bright.
  • Fader board: four ALPS RSA0N11M9 100 mm motorized faders, each with a DRV8871 motor driver, an AT42QT1011 touch sensor, and INSERT and PRE buttons.
  • Front panel: 1.6 mm black anodized aluminium, one 36.3 mm strip per channel.
  • Next revision: a ground beside every signal in the board-to-board cables, and terminated, buffered clock and latch lines for the LED chain.
